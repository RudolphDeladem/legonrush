About LEGONRUSH

What is LEGONRUSH?

LEGONRUSH is a social 3D cycling game that turns campus into a living digital playground. Ride past familiar places, challenge other riders, compete for your hall, discover events and build your own campus identity.

It runs in the browser on any phone or laptop and installs like an app. No download store needed.
