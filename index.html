    Made for campus phones.

    Everything in LEGONRUSH works today, with no account and no app store.

    • Quick Match

      Race riders who are online right now. When nobody is around, three rider bots take their place.

    • Friend challenges

      Send your best run as a link. Your friend races your exact ride and can send theirs back.

    • Ghost runs

      A ghost of your best time rides beside you, with a live gap in seconds.

    • Daily rewards

      Come back each day for coins. A streak takes it from 50 to 250.

    • Highlife on the ride

      A music loop made in the browser, with its own volume.

    • Light on data

      About 500 KB the first time, then it plays offline. No data used while you ride.

    • Smooth on cheap phones

      Graphics switch to smooth mode on their own when a phone struggles.

    • Your settings

      Left-handed boost button, reduced motion and separate sound and music volumes.

    Questions, answered.

    Is LEGONRUSH free?

    Yes. It is free to play, and Rush Coins are earned by riding, never bought.

    Do I need to download anything?

    No. It runs in your browser on a phone, tablet, laptop or desktop. To keep it like an app, tap Install app in the game's You tab on Android, or Share then Add to Home Screen on iPhone.

    How much data does it use?

    About 500 KB on your first visit. After that the game works offline and uses no data while you ride. Updates only download what changed.

    Is it the real campus?

    Yes. The roads and 1,600 buildings come from OpenStreetMap, and the halls, faculties and landmarks use their real names. Directions in Explore follow the real roads.

    Do I need an account?

    No. You can play as a guest straight away, and your progress is saved on your device. Create a free account with your email to keep your progress on any device, post your race times on the leaderboards and count your kilometres for your hall.

    Is it only for University of Ghana students?

    Anyone can ride. It is built for UG students, freshers and anyone visiting Legon who wants to learn their way around.

    Can my brand be in the game?

    Yes. Billboards beside campus roads, sponsored events and branded routes are open to partners. Email legonrush@gmail.com or WhatsApp +233 55 151 9797.
